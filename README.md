Open it at https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/orbit-lab/

# Everett's Alphabet Zoo

Everett's letters app with Keeper Lucy, a human zookeeper. Real animal photos for every letter A to Z (Alligator to Zebra) and a recorded Kokoro voice (Bella) that says each letter's name and sound: "B. B says buh. B is for bear!" Six games: Letter Book (26 stickers), First Letter, Find It, Trace It, Name Builder and Count, each with three levels. Photo credits and a press-and-hold reset are under Grown-ups.

Open it at https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/alphabet-zoo/

# Gizmo Lab

Felix's engineering and design lab in the Sprocket family's cutaway house. Ozzie's Simple Machines backyard (Lever Lab, Pulley Hoist, Ramp Test, Wedge and Screw, Wheel and Axle, plus the ruler-and-pencil seesaw paper mission), Pim's Home Machines kitchen and bathroom (The Flush, The Fridge with Ride the Loop, Keep It Cold and Pack the Fridge, Under the Sink, Locks and Latches, and an on-screen-only look inside the washer and toaster, plus the tank-and-trap paper mission), Rafa's On the Move garage (Gear Lab, Bike Anatomy, and on-screen-only looks at a four-stroke engine and an electric motor, plus the gear-teeth-and-wheel-turns paper mission), Tansy's Take It Apart basement (Exploded View, Parts Drawer and Mystery Gadget, plus the click-pen paper mission) and Jules's Rube Goldberg Workshop (ring the bell, pop the balloon, water the plant, fill the cat's bowl, and free build). Physics by Matter.js (MIT, see gizmo-lab/MATTER-LICENSE.txt), included in the page so it works offline.

Open it at https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/gizmo-lab/

# Culture Lab

The Pen Pal Club: nine kid pen pals plus Wren the pigeon share living cultures (China, India, Thailand, Israel, Morocco, Ghana, France, Mexico, USA). Ten zones: Say It, Write It, Wear It, Mark the Days, Taste It, Pattern It, Play It, Live It, Side by Side and Big Questions. The Mailbag drives it: 26 letters ask Felix for help, he writes back in each pal's script, pays postage, watches Wren fly the letter across the map and collects reply postcards, some with secret words to decode.

Open it at https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/culture-lab/

# History Lab

The Museum After Dark: Felix is a junior curator with Nefertiti the museum cat and an eight-person night crew. Eight rooms (Dig Pit, Hall of Writing, Numbers and Stars, Time Road, Hall of Buildings, Map Room, Exhibit Workshop, Living Cultures wing) and ten Night Cases: find, date, read, trace and label a mystery object, then add it to his own wing with a time-road chart and a map.

Open it at https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/history-lab/

# Chess Lab

Felix's guided chess training on a cream-and-brown marble board: Think Like a Player (strategy), Tactics, Checkmate Nets, Endgame Mastery (with a perfect king-and-pawn coach), Three Simple Openings, and Bonus Openings (French Defense, Danish Gambit). Short lessons with a For Dad panel. Move rules by chess.js (BSD, see chess-lab/CHESS-JS-LICENSE.txt).

Open it at https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/chess-lab/

# Data Lab

Spectrum Works, a rainbow robot workshop for data. Seven bays, one robot each: Tally's Count It, Zola's Ask It (fair surveys), Grid's Table It, Fern's Chart It (with the Chart Studio, Letter Counter and My Charts), Ari's Track It (line graphs and scatter plots), Nia's Crunch It (mean, median, mode, range, outliers) and Vio's Make Sense of It (tricky graphs, chance, cause or coincidence, claim checks). Each bay has three levels and a paper mission. Chief Prism posts a new Data Case every day, and the rainbow meter fills as stamps come in.

Open it at https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/data-lab/

# Time Lab

Portal Mesa, a desert at sunset with a glowing time portal. Seven zones, three levels each (Sand, Mesa, Summit): Clockmaker's Adobe (clocks through history), Sundial Plaza (reading time, a.m. and p.m., 24-hour time, sundials), the Oasis (life spans and tree rings), Night Mesa (the Moon, sunrise and the planets), the Portal (deep time, from Grand Canyon layers to Halley's Comet), the Radio Tower (time zones, the Date Line, Daylight Saving) and Calendar Rock (month lengths, leap years, weekday names, world calendars). Old Tula the tortoise, Dash the roadrunner, Saguaro Sam and Pip the kangaroo rat guide. Paper missions, 27 badges and a grown-ups reset. The Oasis uses real photos (Wikimedia Commons, plus averaged faces from the Face Research Lab London Set for the human card); credits are under Grown-ups.

Open it at https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/time-lab/

# Body Lab

# Wild Lab

Felix's life-science lab in The Seven Domes, a glass conservatory with five keepers (Moss, Nell, Kiki, Teo and Leila). Nine zones: Tree of Life, Cells and Microscopes, Plants (with the Seed Vault Field Guide of 100 plants), Fungi and Microbes, Animals, Habitat Tour, Adaptations and Evolution, Food Webs and Wild Watch. 60 stations at three levels, 180 stars, a paper mission in every zone and a press-and-hold grown-ups reset. # Energy Lab

Felix's energy lab in The Powerhouse, the building that powers the town of Brightwater. Energy is never made or used up; it only changes form, and Joule (in six forms) shows how. Nine rooms: The Big Idea, Sun, Wind, Water, Earth Heat, Food and Muscle, Ancient Sunlight (coal, oil and gas), Atom Power and Electricity, plus Power the Town, where Felix mixes power sources to keep the town lit through sun, cloud, calm and a heat wave. 60 stations at three levels (Candle, Lantern, Lighthouse), 180 stars, animated labs, a paper mission in every room and a press-and-hold grown-ups reset. Every source is shown by what it does well and what it costs. Two files: `index.html` and `content.js` (all stations and missions).

Open it at https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/energy-lab/

# Launchers

One home-screen icon per boy that opens all his apps. Each app opens in its own window; tap Done to come back. Press and hold the gear for the grown-ups area (show or hide apps on that iPad, links to every app).

- Felix: https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/felix/
- Everett: https://bwstroup.github.io/word-lab/everett/

To add an app, add a row to `launcher/build_launchers.py`, run `python3 launcher/build_launchers.py`, and commit.
